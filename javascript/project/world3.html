Civilizations & Lore
🌌
The Vel'Shara
Spirit-weavers who exist simultaneously in all seven dimensions. They communicate across the veil using songs composed not of sound but of pure intention — each note a bridge between what is and what might be.
📖
The Veil Codex
A book that writes itself, recording futures yet to happen. The Vel'Shara guard it fiercely — reading your own future is strictly forbidden, for it collapses all alternate paths into one inescapable fate.
🌀
The Hollow Gate
An ancient doorway made of crystallized memories floating in the center of the realm. Every soul that has ever lived has passed through it — before birth, and again after death.
✨
The Forgetting
A catastrophic event when all memory vanished from Umbra'veil for 100 years. No one knows what caused it. Those alive during it remember nothing — not even themselves. The echoes remain.
