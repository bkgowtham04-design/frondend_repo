Civilizations & Lore
🌊
The Thal'Mari
The ancient rulers of Aqua'lon who built their cities from living coral and bioluminescent crystals. They communicate through harmonic frequencies that travel thousands of miles through the water.
🔮
The Tide Codex
A sacred text written in patterns of bioluminescence, believed to hold the secret names of all three moons. Only the High Weavers can read it without losing their minds.
🏛️
City of Lum'Ara
The capital city, suspended midway between the surface and the abyss. Its towers made of living sea-glass glow with the memories of a thousand generations of inhabitants.
⚡
The Great Current
A river of pure energy that flows through the deepest trenches, said to be the heartbeat of the planet itself. Those who touch it gain visions of past and future.
