Civilizations & Lore
🔥
The Kar'Veth
Forge-born warriors who bathe in lava rivers as a rite of passage. Their armor is crafted from solidified star-metal that fell during the Ember Rain of ten thousand years past.
📜
The Ash Scrolls
Ancient knowledge written in the soot of a dying god, etched into volcanic obsidian. Only the Flame Readers can decipher its warnings — few who try survive the reading.
🏔️
Mount Khar'Vul
The world's highest volcano and sacred birthplace of the Kar'Veth. Its peak pierces the atmosphere and is said to touch the dying sun that watches over Ignar'os with silent rage.
💥
The Ember War
A century-long conflict between the sky clans and the deep-earth burrowers that ended when both sides fell into the Great Caldera — and somehow became one unified people of fire.
